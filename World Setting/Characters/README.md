# Characters Directory - Chronicles of Destiny

This directory contains detailed character profiles for all significant characters in the Chronicles of Destiny series, organized by role and importance.

## Directory Structure

```
Characters/
├── README.md (this file)
├── Main/                    # Protagonists and central characters
├── Supporting/              # Important allies and secondary characters

```

## Character Relationships

### The Trinity
The legendary fighting unit consisting of Kaelen, Lyra, and Drakon. Their bond is unbreakable and they fight as coordinated unit.

### Romantic Relationships (Book 1)
- **Kaelen & Elfiria:** Deep romantic love; Elfiria's death profoundly impacts Kaelen
- **Lyra's Unrequited Love:** Lyra loved Kaelen but maturely resolved feelings into deep friendship

### Mentor-Student
- **Aric → Kaelen:** Aric trained Kaelen and awakened his Sword Saint potential
- **Aric → The Vanguard:** Trained elite fighters of the Alliance

### Leadership Structure - The Exiled
- **Commander:** Kaelen
- **Second-in-Command:** Lyra (tactical coordinator)
- **Key Commanders:** Elder Grimm, Drakon, Captain Theron
- **Fighters:** Gorim and others

## Key Factions

### The Exiled (Outer Lands Resistance)
**Leadership:** Kaelen (Commander), Lyra (Second-in-Command), Elder Grimm, Drakon, Captain Theron
**Size:** 1,500 fighters (as of end Book 1)
**Base:** Deephollow
**Mission:** Protect outer lands refugees; resist demon forces

### Alliance of Light (Within Holy Dome)
**Leaders:** King Aldric (Eldoria), Princess Seraphina (Sylvanaria), others
**Protected Kingdoms:** Aetheria, Eldoria, Sylvanaria, Drakoria
**Status:** Rebuilding within Holy Dome protection

### Demon Army
**Supreme Leader:** The Demon King
**Commanders:** Seven Demon Generals (5 active/recovering, 1 dead, 2 unknown)
**Territory:** All outer lands beyond Holy Dome
