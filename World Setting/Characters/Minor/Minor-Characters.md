# Minor Characters - Book 1

This file documents named minor characters who appear in Book 1 but have limited roles.

---

## Horvath

**Category:** Minor Supporting Character
**Status:** Presumed alive (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Race:** Human
- **Role:** Merchant; Caravan master
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 1

### Description
- Well-dressed in merchant finery
- Face like wrinkled leather
- Sharp, calculating eyes
- Surprisingly competent fighter
- Good judge of character

### Story Role
- Hired Kaelen, Gorim, and others for caravan escort (Chapter 1)
- Cargo was magical components for kingdom defense
- Later served as merchant guild liaison

### Skills
- Merchant negotiations
- Basic swordsmanship
- Logistics and supply management

## General Marcus

**Category:** Minor Character - Important Witness
**Status:** Survived (as of Chapter 3)

### Basic Information
- **Race:** Human
- **Role:** General of Eldoria's Third Army
- **Experience:** 30-year military veteran
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 3

### Description
- **Condition (Ch 3):** Broken in body and spirit, crawling away from the battlefield
- **Traits:** Survivor, traumatized by the demon's return

### Story Role
- Commanded forces at Aridia/Eldoria border
- Witnessed Demon King's resurrection ritual firsthand
- Survived demon ritual that killed most of his army
- Delivered warning to Eldoria about demonic return

### Importance
- One of first witnesses to Demon King's resurrection
- Testimony confirmed the threat to Alliance

## Notes on Minor Characters

Many soldiers, civilians, and background characters appear throughout Book 1 without names:
- Alliance soldiers and commanders
- Refugees from fallen kingdoms
- Tavern patrons
- Merchant guild members
- Mages' Guild members
- Palace guards and servants
- Demon horde (unnamed demons)

These unnamed characters populate the world but are not individually documented unless they have significant story impact.
