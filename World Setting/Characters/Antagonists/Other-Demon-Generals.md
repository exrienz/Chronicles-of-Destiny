# Other Demon Generals

This file documents the remaining Demon Generals from Book 1.

---

## General Xylos - Herald of Suffering

**Status:** Alive but wounded (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of Suffering
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 3

### Abilities
- **Pain Curse:** Can inflict supernatural agony
- **Suffering Aura:** Amplifies all pain and wounds in area
- **Combat Skills:** Skilled demon warrior
- **Void Magic:** Shadow/void magical abilities

### Major Events
- Chapter 3: Appeared after Demon King's resurrection
- Chapter 8: Helped kill Aric; participated in five-on-one battle
- Chapter 10+: Fought during Temple of Stars battle
- Briefly captured by Drakon but escaped
- Later wounded but still active

### Status at End of Book 1
- **Condition:** Wounded but recovering
- **Location:** Outer lands
- **Activity:** Still serving Demon King

## General Dravos - Herald of War

**Status:** Alive (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of War
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 8

### Physical Appearance
- Armored in bone and shadow
- Radiates war energy

### Abilities
- **War Aura:** Makes combat more deadly for all combatants
- **Soul Blade:** Weapon leaves screaming soul afterimages
- **Combat Mastery:** Centuries of warfare experience
- **Respects Warriors:** Honors worthy opponents

### Major Events
- Chapter 8: Helped kill Aric in five-on-one battle
- Led assault on Ironhold fortress
- Fought Kaelen directly
- Active in major demon campaigns

### Status at End of Book 1
- **Condition:** Healthy and active
- **Location:** Outer lands
- **Activity:** Leading demon campaigns

## General Vex - Herald of Plague

**Status:** Critically wounded (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of Plague
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 3

### Abilities
- **Plague Breath:** Miasma that melts flesh
- **Disease Manipulation:** Creates and spreads supernatural plagues
- **Corruption:** Can corrupt living beings
- **Void Magic:** Shadow/void abilities

### Major Events
- Chapter 3: Appeared after Demon King's resurrection
- Chapter 8: Helped kill Aric in five-on-one battle
- Critically wounded by Aric's final Storm of Blades attack
- Still recovering from wounds

### Status at End of Book 1
- **Condition:** Critically wounded; recovering slowly
- **Location:** Unknown, likely demon territory
- **Threat Level:** Reduced due to injuries

## General Noxia - Herald of Shadows

**Status:** DECEASED (killed Chapter 8)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of Shadows
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 3
- **Death:** Book 1, Chapter 8

### Abilities
- **Shadow Magic:** Master of shadow manipulation
- **Binding Spells:** Could trap and immobilize enemies
- **Void Powers:** High-level shadow/void magic

### Major Events
- Chapter 3: Appeared after Demon King's resurrection
- Chapter 8: Participated in killing Aric alongside other Generals
- **Chapter 8 - Death:** Killed by Aric's final Storm of Blades technique during his last stand

### Legacy
- First Demon General confirmed killed in Book 1
- Death proved Generals can be defeated
- Aric's final act of heroism
