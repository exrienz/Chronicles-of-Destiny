# Chapter 13: Seccubus Revealed

## Part 1: The Circle Tightens

The Ritual Chamber pulsed with a heartbeat that wasn't human.

Finriel stood between the two altars—one holding Elfiria’s white bones, the other holding Thalia’s trembling body.

"Begin," Seccubus commanded from the shadows.

Finriel raised her hands. She was trembling too. The energy in the room was suffocating. It felt heavy, like being underwater at the bottom of the ocean.

"Spiritus... Revertus..."

She began the chant.

Violet light flowed from her hands. It wrapped around Elfiria's bones, lifting them into the air. The bones began to glow. Small cracks appeared in the white calcium, leaking light.

"It's working," Finriel whispered. "She's waking up."

She turned the flow of magic towards the second altar. Towards Thalia.

"Connect," she commanded.

A tendril of violet light snake out and touched Thalia’s chest.

Thalia gasped. Her back arched. Her eyes rolled back.

"Fin..." Thalia moaned. "It hurts."

FInriel flinched. "It hurts? Seccubus, you said it was just a nap!"

"The connection must be forged," Seccubus said smoothly. "Discomfort is natural. Keep going."

Finriel hesitated. Thalia wasn't just uncomfortable. She was pale. Her skin was turning grey, just like the elf prisoner Finriel had drained.

"No," Finriel pulled back. "Stop. Something is wrong."

"Nothing is wrong," Seccubus stepped forward. his eyes were hard. "Do not break the circle, Finriel. If you stop now, the backlash will kill them both."

"Kill them?" Finriel panicked. "You didn't say that!"

"Power has consequences!" Seccubus snapped. "Continue! Channel the energy! Feed the bones!"

Finriel looked at Thalia. Thalia was looking at her. Her eyes were terrified.

*Please,* Thalia mouthed. *Stop.*

Finriel looked at Elfiria's bones. They were vibrating. A ghostly shape was starting to form—a silhouette of light.

*Finriel...* the ghost whispered. *Help me...*

Finriel was torn. The two voices she loved most. One dead, one living.

"I... I can't let her go," Finriel whispered.

She poured more mana into the spell.

"Just a little longer, Thal," she promised. "I'll fix it. I'll fix everything."

Thalia screamed.

***

## Part 2: The Breakers

Outside the Spire, the storm was raging.

Kaelen and Drakon charged through the obsidian gates. They were alone. No army. Just two broken legends against a legion of shadows.

"FOR ELFIRIA!" Kaelen roared, swinging a new weapon—a massive warhammer he had taken from the armory. It wasn't *World-Breaker*, but it shattered stone just fine.

He smashed a Shadow-Knight into mist.

"FOR THE PRINCESS!" Drakon bellowed, breathing a cone of fire that turned the mist to steam.

They fought back-to-back.

"They're endless!" Drakon grunted, taking a spear to the shoulder. He snapped the shaft without flinching.

"We just need to reach the stairs!" Kaelen shouted. "Lyra! Cover us!"

High above, hidden in the cliffs, Lyra lay prone with a sniper rifle—a relic of the old wars.

*Bang.*

A wraith looming over Kaelen’s head exploded.

*Bang.*

Another one disintegrated.

"Move!" Lyra's voice crackled in their earpieces. "I can't hold them forever!"

Kaelen and Drakon charged the stairs.

They fought their way up the spiral. Floor by floor. Room by room.

They were bleeding. Exhausted.

"I'm getting too old for this," Drakon wheezed, leaning against a wall on the 50th floor.

"You're only three hundred," Kaelen spat blood. "Stop whining."

They reached the doors of the Ritual Chamber.

They were sealed with violet runes.

"Finriel's work," Kaelen realized. "It's a Void Lock."

"Can we break it?" Drakon asked.

Kaelen hefted his hammer.

"We can knock," he growled.

He slammed the hammer into the door.

*BOOM.*

The runes flared. The door didn't budge. But the stone around it cracked.

"Again!"

*BOOM.*

Inside, Finriel heard the pounding.

"Someone is here," she said, distracting from the chant.

"Ignore them!" Seccubus yelled. He wasn't smiling anymore. He looked hungry. "FINISH IT!"

***

## Part 3: The Revelation

Finriel looked at the small altar.

Thalia wasn't screaming anymore. She was silent. Her skin was translucent.

"She's fading," Finriel realized with horror. "Seccubus... she's fading!"

"She is the Catalyst!" Seccubus laughed. The sound was wrong. It was multiple voices overlapping. "The bridge must be burned to cross the river, Finriel! Did you really think you could cheat death for free?"

"You lied to me!" Finriel severed the connection. She pulled the violet light back.

The spell destabilized. The ghost of Elfiria flickered and vanished. The bones fell back into the coffin with a clatter.

"What have you done?!" Seccubus roared. His human disguise melted away.

He grew. His skin turned to obsidian plates. Wings of shadow erupted from his back. His eyes became burning coals.

This was his true form. A Demon General.

"I stopped," Finriel said, standing between Seccubus and Thalia. "I won't trade her."

"You already have!" Seccubus sneered. "Look at her!"

Finriel turned.

Thalia was breathing. But barely. Her hair had turned white. She looked... aged. Drained.

"Thalia?" Finriel shook her. "Wake up."

Thalia didn't wake up.

"She is stuck," Seccubus explained, stepping closer. "Halfway between life and death. You started the transfer, Finriel. Her soul is wandering the Void. If you don't finish the ritual... she stays there forever. A ghost in the machine."

"No..." Finriel sobbed.

"Finish it!" Seccubus commanded. "Sacrifice the rest of her! Bring Elfiria back! At least then one of them lives! If you stop now... you lose both."

It was a nightmare logic. But it made terrible sense.

Finriel looked at the bones. Then at Thalia.

"I can't," she whispered.

*BOOM.*

The doors exploded inward.

Kaelen and Drakon stormed in.

"Get away from her!" Kaelen yelled.

He charged Seccubus, swinging the hammer.

Seccubus caught the hammer with one hand.

"Ah," the Demon General smiled. "The audience has arrived."

He flicked his wrist.

Kaelen was thrown across the room. He hit the wall with a sickening crunch.

Drakon roared and breathed fire.

Seccubus inhaled the fire. He literally ate it.

"Spicy," Seccubus mocked. He backhanded Drakon. The massive Dragon-Kin flew through a pillar, burying him in rubble.

Seccubus turned back to Finriel.

"See?" he gestured to the broken heroes. "They are weak. They cannot save you. They cannot save her. Only *you* can."

He pointed to Thalia.

"Make your choice, Chosen One. The Friend... or the Legend?"

Finriel looked at Kaelen, bleeding on the floor.

"Kaelen!" she cried. "Help me!"

Kaelen looked up. His eye was swollen shut. He saw Thalia on the altar. He saw the white hair.

"Finriel..." he rasped. "Don't... don't listen..."

"She's dying, Kaelen!" Finriel screamed. "He says if I don't finish it, she's gone forever!"

"He lies!" Kaelen coughed. "The Void alway lies! Thalia is still in there! Reach for her! Not with magic... with *you*!"

"With me?" Finriel looked at her hands. They were glowing violet.

"I don't know who 'me' is anymore," she whispered.

"Touching," Seccubus sneered. "But time is up."

He raised his hand.

"If you won't finish the ritual... I will."

He aimed a blast of void energy at Thalia.

"NO!" Finriel screamed.

She jumped in front of the blast.

She shielded Thalia with her body.

The blast hit her.

It didn't hurt.

It *absolved* her.

***

## Part 4: The Void Avatar

The energy didn't kill Finriel. She absorbed it.

Her eyes went white. Then black. Then violet.

She began to float.

"You want a monster?" Finriel’s voice echoed. It sounded like a legion. "I'll show you a monster."

She reached out.

The Spire shook. The stone walls cracked.

"Gravitas... OBLIVION."

She clenched her fist.

The air around Seccubus compressed. The demon roared as gravity increased a thousandfold. The floor beneath him turned to dust. His obsidian armor cracked.

"Impossible!" Seccubus strained against the weight. "You are just a vessel!"

"I am the Ocean," Finriel said.

She slammed him down. Again. And again.

*Boom. Boom. Boom.*

Seccubus was pounded into the floor. He was flattened. Broken.

Finally, he lay still. A pile of shattered obsidian and black ichor.

Finriel lowered her hand. She stared at the ruin of the General.

"Is he dead?" Kaelen whispered, pulling himself up.

Finriel floated down. She landed beside Thalia.

She ignored Kaelen. She touched Thalia's face.

"Thal?"

Thalia opened her eyes. They were milky white. Blind.

"Fin?" Thalia whispered. Her voice was thin. "It's dark."

"I know," Finriel said, tears streaming down her face. "I'm here. I stopped him."

"Did you... did you save her?" Thalia asked. "Elfiria?"

Finriel looked at the pile of bones. They were just bones.

"No," Finriel whispered.

"That's okay," Thalia smiled weakly. "At least... you're safe."

"Thalia?" Finriel squeezed her hand. "Stay with me."

"I'm tired, Fin," Thalia said. "I think... I think I'll sleep now."

"No!" Finriel panicked. "Don't sleep! Stay awake!"

"Just a nap," Thalia murmured.

She closed her eyes.

Her chest stopped moving.

Finriel waited. One second. Two. Ten.

"Thalia?"

Silence.

Finriel looked at Kaelen.

"Fix her," she commanded.

Kaelen limped over. He checked for a pulse.

He shook his head.

"She's gone, Fin."

"NO!" Finriel screamed. The room shook. "FIX HER! YOU'RE A HERO! DO SOMETHING!"

"I can't," Kaelen whispered. "She gave her soul to the ritual. There's nothing to call back."

Finriel stared at Thalia's body. The white hair. The aged face.

She had done this. She had brought her here. She had cast the spell.

"I killed her," Finriel whispered.

"Seccubus killed her," Kaelen said, trying to touch Finriel's shoulder.

Finriel recoiled. Violet flames erupted from her skin.

"DON'T TOUCH ME!"

She backed away.

"I killed her. I killed the driver. I killed the Stalker. I kill everything I touch."

She looked at Seccubus's corpse.

The corpse... laughed.

"Well done," the pile of rubble whispered. It dissolved into smoke, reforming near the door. Seccubus was smaller, weaker, but alive. "You broke the seal, Finriel. You killed the Anchor."

"You..." Finriel turned to him.

"You are ready now," Seccubus said. "The pain... it is exquisite, isn't it? Let it in. Let it fill you. You don't need friends. You have Power."

Finriel looked at him. Then at Kaelen. Then at Thalia.

She screamed.

It was a scream that shattered the stained glass windows. A scream that cracked the Spire.

She grabbed *Dawnbringer*.

"I will kill you," she promised Seccubus. "I will kill you and burn your soul."

Seccubus bowed. "I look forward to it. Come find me... in the North."

He vanished in a cloud of smoke.

Finriel stood alone in the ruins of the chamber.

Kaelen reached out. "Fin... come home."

Finriel looked at him. Her eyes were empty black pools.

"Home?" she whispered. "I have no home."

She picked up Thalia's body. Effortlessly.

"I am taking her," she said.

"Where?"

"Away from you," Finriel said. "Away from this war."

She walked to the window.

"Finriel, don't!" Kaelen shouted.

Finriel stepped out.

She didn't fall. She flew. A streak of violet darkness against the storm.

Kaelen watched her go. He fell to his knees beside the empty coffin of Elfiria.

"I lost her, El," he wept. "I lost them both."

And outside, the storm finally ended. But the darkness had just begun.
