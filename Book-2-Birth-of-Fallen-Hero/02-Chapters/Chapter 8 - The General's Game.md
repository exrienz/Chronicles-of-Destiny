# Chapter 8: The General's Game

## Part 1: The Calm Before

Finriel returned to her bunk before dawn. She smelled of ozone and shadow, but a quick scrubbing with cold water hid the scent.

Her mind was buzzing.

Seccubus hadn't taught her a spell. He had taught her a *perspective*.

*"Pain is energy,"* he had whispered, his voice still echoing in her ears. *"Fear is fuel. The Light rejects these things. The Void consumes them. Be the consumer, not the meal."*

She looked at her hands. She felt... steady. For the first time since arriving in Deephollow, the shaking had stopped.

Thalia was asleep in her new bunk down the hall. Finriel peeked in. Thalia looked peaceful.

"I can protect you now," Finriel whispered. "I know how."

"Fin?"

Finriel spun around.

Kaelen stood at the end of the corridor. He was fully armored, helmet under his arm. He looked like he hadn't slept in a week.

"Commander," Finriel stiffened.

"You were out," Kaelen stated. It wasn't a question.

"I needed air," Finriel lied smoothly. "The barracks are stifling."

Kaelen studied her. He looked tailored to find lies, but Finriel’s newfound calm was a perfect shield.

"The wind is shifting," Kaelen said eventually. "Dravos's legion is moving. Malakor has been spotted near the Ash River. Something is coming."

"Let them come," Finriel said. "We're ready."

Kaelen narrowed his eye. "You seem... confident. After yesterday."

"I did a lot of thinking," Finriel said. "You were right. About survival. I need to be harder."

Kaelen didn't look relieved. He looked worried. "Harder isn't always better, Finriel. Stone breaks. Iron rusts. Sometimes you need to be flexible."

"Water drowns people, Commander," Finriel countered. "I'm going to the armory. If we're fighting, I need arrows."

She walked past him.

Kaelen watched her go. He touched the hilt of *World-Breaker*.

*She's changed,* he thought. *Overnight. The fear is gone. But what replaced it?*

## Part 2: The Red Horizon

The attack didn't start with a horn. It started with the ground shaking.

Finriel was on the East Wall duty, sharpening a sword she had borrowed (after Grog mocked her shiny one), when the tea in her cup started to ripple.

*Boom. Boom. Boom.*

"Earthquake?" Thalia asked, clutching a basket of bandages. She was assigned to runner duty for the healers.

"No," Finriel stood up, looking at the horizon. "Marching."

The grey mist of the Dead Zone parted.

A sea of black armor emerged. Thousands. Ten thousand? No... more. It stretched from the canyon walls to the horizon line.

Demons. Not just Scavengers or Horrors. Organized ranks of Fel-Guard—demons in heavy jagged plate holding massive obsidian shields. Behind them, Siege-Beasts—lumbering giants with catapults strapped to their backs. And above them... a swarm of Harpies blocking out the weak sun.

"Gods above," a soldier next to her whispered. "It's the Legion."

A horn blew. A deep, mournful sound that rattled Finriel's teeth.

"ALARM!" Kaelen's voice roared over the magical amplifiers. "ALL HANDS TO WALLS! THIS IS NOT A DRILL! SHIELD WALL FORMATION!"

The city exploded into chaos. Soldiers ran to stations. Civilians fled to the deep caves.

"Thalia, go to the caves," Finriel ordered, grabbing her friend's shoulder.

"I'm a runner!" Thalia argued, terrified but stubborn. "The healers need me!"

"You'll die up here!" Finriel yelled. "look at them!"

"Everyone dies up here!" Thalia yelled back. "That's the job! You fight, I run! Go!"

She shoved Finriel and ran towards the medical tent.

Finriel watched her go.

*Protect her,* Aria whispered. *Kill them all.*

"I will," Finriel promised.

She ran to her post on the main gate tower. Kaelen, Lyra, and Drakon were already there.

"They brought Siege-Beasts," Drakon growled. "They mean to knock the door down."

"Target the giants first," Kaelen ordered. "Ballistae! Focus fire on the big uglies! Mages! Shield dome on my mark!"

Below, the demon legion stopped.

A single figure rode out on a nightmare-steed. He was massive, wearing armor made of dragon skulls. He carried a glaive that pulsed with green fel-fire.

General Malakor.

Malakor raised his glaive. He pointed it at the gate.

"OPEN," his voice boomed, magically amplified to shake the stones. "OR BLEED."

"NUTS!" Drakon shouted back.

Malakor lowered his glaive. "Bleed."

The Siege-Beasts roared. Catapults fired. Massive balls of green fire arced through the sky.

"INCOMING!"

## Part 3: The Siege

The battle was a blur of noise and heat.

Finriel stood on the ramparts, blasting.

"Fulminos!"

Lightning arced from *Dawnbringer*, striking a cluster of Harpies. They fell like burning stones.

"Ignis!"

Fireballs exploded among the Fel-Guard ranks. But their obsidian shields absorbed the magic. They kept marching.

*They are resistant to elemental magic,* Seccubus’s voice whispered in her mind. *Use force. Use gravity.*

Finriel hesitated. Gravity was risky. She had almost crushed Drakon.

"Just aim better," she told herself.

She focused on a Siege-Beast lumbering towards the gate. It was huge, sixty feet tall. Arrows bounced off its hide.

"Gravitas!" Finriel thrust her hand out.

She visualized the air above the beast becoming lead.

The beast stumbled. Its knees buckled. It roared in confusion as its own weight crushed it into the dirt. The catapult on its back misfired, launching a fireball into its own ranks.

"Good shot!" Grog yelled from a nearby ballista. "Do it again!"

Finriel grinned. She felt powerful.

But there were too many of them.

The Fel-Guard reached the walls. They threw ladders—made of bone and sinew that gripped the stone. Demons swarmed up like ants.

"They're breaching Sector 7!" Lyra’s voice came over the comms crystal. "Review! We need backup!"

"Finriel!" Kaelen yelled, parrying a demon sword. "Take Grog's squad! Plug the breach at Sector 7! Go!"

"On it!"

Finriel jumped off the tower—a forty foot drop. She used a wind-cushion to land softly.

"ON ME!" she screamed. Grog and ten others rallied to her.

They ran through the streets. The breach was chaotic. A section of the wall had collapsed. Demons were pouring into the lower district—where the civilian shelters were.

"Hold the line!" Finriel ordered. "Don't let them past the bakery!"

"Bakery?" Grog laughed nervously. "It's a butcher shop now!"

A massive demon—a Mauler, all muscle and teeth—charged them.

Grog stepped up, shield raised. "Come get some, ugly!"

The Mauler slammed into Grog. The impact sent the half-orc flying through a window.

"Grog!" Finriel screamed.

The line broke. Demons flooded the street.

Finriel was surrounded. She spun, slashing with *Dawnbringer*.

*Slash. Parry. Blast.*

She killed three. But five more took their place.

She was tired. The adrenaline was fading. Her mana was dipping.

*Drink,* Seccubus whispered. *They are full of life. Take it.*

Finriel felt the temptation. The violet rune in her mind pulsed.

*No,* she thought. *Not here. Kaelen will see.*

A scream cut through the noise.

"HELP!"

Finriel looked.

Down the alley, Thalia was cornered. She had been running supplies. Now she was backed against a wall by two Maulers. She was swinging her frying pan wildly.

"Stay back!" Thalia shrieked.

One Mauler grabbed the pan and tossed it aside. It reached for her.

"THALIA!"

Finriel turned to run to her. But a Fel-Guard blocked her path. He slammed his shield into her face.

Finriel went down. Stars exploded in her vision. *Dawnbringer* slid away.

She looked up, dazed. She saw the Mauler grab Thalia by the throat. Thalia’s feet kicked the air.

"No..." Finriel wheezed. She tried to stand. Her legs wouldn't work.

The Fel-Guard raised his sword to finish her.

*She is going to die,* Seccubus said. *Your friend. Your anchor. She dies now. Unless...*

Time seemed to freeze.

Finriel looked at Thalia's purple face. She looked at the Fel-Guard's sword.

She had a choice.

Die honorably. Or live monstrously.

"I choose life," she whispered.

She didn't reach for the Light. She reached for the Void. She tore the door off the hinges.

"NOOOOO!"

Finriel screamed.

It wasn't a scream of fear. It was a scream of command.

A shockwave of violet energy exploded from her body.

The Fel-Guard evaporated. He didn't burn. He simply ceased to exist, turning into fine grey dust.

The shockwave rippled out. It hit the Maulers holding Thalia.

*CRUNCH.*

They were flattened against the wall, crushed instantly into paste.

Thalia fell to the ground, gasping.

The shockwave kept going. It hit the surrounding buildings. Stone cracked. Glass shattered.

In the center of the devastation, Finriel stood up.

She wasn't glowing green anymore. She was wreathed in violet flame. Her eyes were solid black pools. Her hair floated as if underwater.

She looked at the remaining demons in the street.

They stopped. They looked at her. And for the first time in history... the demons looked afraid.

"Run," Finriel said. Her voice was layered—hers, and something deeper.

The demons ran.

## Part 4: The Aftermath

The battle ended at sunset. The demons retreated, leaving thousands of dead at the walls.

Deephollow had survived. Barely.

Finriel sat on a crate in the ruined alley of Sector 7. The violet flame was gone. She was just a girl again. Shaking. covered in dust.

Kaelen walked into the alley. He stepped over the pile of demon paste that used to be Maulers. He looked at the dust shadow where the Fel-Guard had stood.

He looked at Finriel.

"What happened here?" he asked quietly.

"I saved her," Finriel whispered.

Thalia was sitting nearby, wrapped in a blanket, staring at Finriel. She wasn't thanking her. She was shivering.

"Finriel," Kaelen walked closer. "This... destruction. It's not Light magic."

"Does it matter?" Finriel looked up. Her eyes were clear again, but hard. "They're dead. Thalia is alive. The breach is plugged."

"You used the Void," Kaelen accuse. "I can smell it. It smells like ozone and grave dirt."

"I used what I had!" Finriel stood up. "I saved this city! You should be thanking me!"

"Thanking you?!" Kaelen grabbed her shoulders. "You opened a Void Gate in the middle of a populated area! Look at the walls, Finriel! Look at the cracks! You weakened reality itself!"

"I SAVED THEM!" Finriel shoved him away. She was strong. Stronger than before. Kaelen stumbled back.

"You are dangerous," Kaelen said, his hand straying to his sword. "I told Lyra. You are a bomb."

"Then maybe you should stop lighting matches!" Finriel yelled.

"Commander!"

Drakon limped into the alley, clutching his side. "Malakor is retreating. But... he left a message."

"What message?" Kaelen asked, not taking his eyes off Finriel.

"He said 'The Vessel is ripening'," Drakon said.

Kaelen went pale.

He looked at Finriel. Really looked at her.

"You're grounded," Kaelen said. " confinement. Tower 4. You don't leave until I say so. You don't cast magic until I say so."

"You can't lock me up!" Finriel protested.

"Watch me," Kaelen signaled. Two guards stepped forward. They looked terrified, but they obeyed. "Escort Lady Finriel to the tower. If she tries to cast... subdue her."

Finriel looked at the guards. She could kill them. With a thought. It would be so easy.

*Do it,* Aria whispered. *They are jailors.*

Finriel looked at Thalia. Thalia was watching her with wide, fearful eyes.

*If I hurt them, I lose her forever,* Finriel realized.

"Fine," Finriel spat. "I'll go."

She walked past Kaelen.

"You are making a mistake, old man," she whispered. "You need me."

"I need a hero," Kaelen said sadly. "Not a demon."

Finriel laughed. It was a cold, sharp sound.

"Same thing."

## Part 5: The Tower

Tower 4 was a solitary confinement cell high on the spire. It had a nice view of the devastation.

Finriel sat on the bed. The door was locked. Runes of suppression were etched into the walls, dampening her access to mana.

She couldn't reach the Light. It was blocked.

But she could hear the Void. The runes couldn't block that. The Void was everywhere.

*He betrayed you,* Seccubus whispered. *After you saved his city.*

"He's scared," Finriel said to the empty room.

*He is jealous. He knows you are stronger than Elfiria ever was. He wants to keep you weak so he can be the big strong protector.*

*But we know the truth. Malakor is coming back. Next time, he brings the King.*

"I can't fight the King from a cell," Finriel said.

*Then leave,* Seccubus suggested. *The walls are stone. Stone obeys gravity. You know the spell.*

Finriel looked at the wall. She could blow it out. Fly away. Go to the wilds.

"And go where?"

*To me. I can teach you the rest. I can teach you how to break the suppression runes. How to summon the Black Fire. How to bring the dead back... properly.*

Finriel froze. "Properly?"

*Garret,* Seccubus whispered. *You botched it. But I can show you how to fix him. How to give him his memories back.*

"You can do that?"

*I can do anything, little bird. I am the Herald.*

Finriel stood up. She walked to the window.

"Teach me," she said.

*Excellent. First... we need to get rid of the guards.*

Finriel looked at the door.

"No killing," she said. "Not yet."

*As you wish. Sleep spell then. But a deep one.*

Finriel placed her hand on the door. She reached for the Void, bypassing the suppression runes by pulling from the shadows in the corner of the room.

Violet light pulsed.

"Sleep," she commanded.

Outside, two thuds echoed as the guards collapsed.

Finriel focused on the lock. *Click.*

She opened the door.

She was free.

And she wasn't going back to the Light.
