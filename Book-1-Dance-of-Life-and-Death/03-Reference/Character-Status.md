## MAIN CHARACTERS

### The Trinity (Alive - Intact)

**Kaelen** - ALIVE
- **Status:** Commander of The Exiled
- **Location:** Deephollow, outer lands
- **Condition:** Healthy, battle-hardened
- **Power Level:** Awakening Sword Saint abilities (silver energy, enhanced combat)
- **Relationships:**
  - Lover of Elfiria (deceased, grieving)
  - Best friends with Lyra and Drakon
  - Leads 1,500 fighters
- **Equipment:** Twin swords (dual-wielding)
- **Emotional State:** Grieving Elfiria's death but committed to her legacy
- **Threat Status:** Personally noticed by Demon King

**Lyra** - ALIVE
- **Status:** Second-in-Command of The Exiled; Tactical Coordinator
- **Location:** Deephollow, outer lands
- **Condition:** Healthy
- **Power Level:** Expert sniper/ranger
- **Relationships:**
  - Resolved unrequited feelings for Kaelen into deep friendship
  - Battle-sister with Drakon
  - Key leader in The Exiled
- **Equipment:** Enchanted Longbow of Seeking
- **Emotional State:** At peace, fulfilled by purpose
- **Development:** Matured significantly, handles complex emotions with grace

**Drakon** - ALIVE
- **Status:** Key Commander of The Exiled
- **Location:** Deephollow, outer lands
- **Condition:** Healthy
- **Power Level:** Dragon Knight with full transformation capability
- **Relationships:**
  - Battle-brother to Kaelen ("Tiny Leader") and Lyra ("Small Elf")
  - Emotional anchor of The Trinity
- **Equipment:** Heavy plate armor, massive shield
- **Emotional State:** Matured but maintains essential optimism
- **Development:** From comic relief to wise warrior-prince

### Other Main Characters

**Elfiria** - DECEASED (Chapter 14)
- **Death:** Overused divine power for six months helping refugees; life force drained
- **Final Location:** Buried at Deephollow outside Holy Dome
- **Legacy:**
  - Holy Dome remains active (protects four kingdoms)
  - The Exiled founded on her principles
  - Her sacrifice inspires resistance movement
- **Relationship:** Kaelen's deceased lover; her memory drives his mission

**Aric** - DECEASED (Chapter 8)
- **Death:** Killed fighting five Demon Generals at Valley of Kings
- **Final Act:** Killed General Noxia, critically wounded General Vex with Storm of Blades
- **Legacy:**
  - Trained Kaelen and awakened his Sword Saint potential
  - His teachings continue through The Exiled
  - Death inspired Alliance despite devastating loss
