## CRITICAL CONTINUITY FIXES

### 1. Demon Timeline Paradox (Chapter 2)
**Issue**: Chapter 2 showed demons attacking before Chapter 3's seal breaking
**Fix**: Changed Chapter 2 enemies from "demons" to "Void-Corrupted abominations"
- Updated descriptions to "corrupted beasts", "Void-Corrupted creatures", "twisted by dark magic"
- Changed dialogue to reference "Shadow cultist experiments" instead of true demons
- This preserves the surprise reveal of true demons in Chapter 3

**Files Modified**:
- `Chapter 2 - The Domino Falls.md` (lines 316-420)

### 2. Demon General Count & Status (Chapters 3, 8, 9)
**Issue**: Inconsistent tracking of 7 Demon Generals across chapters
**Fix**: Created definitive status tracking document
- Noxia: DECEASED (killed by Aric, Ch 8)
- Vex: WOUNDED (injured by Aric, Ch 8)
- Malakor: ACTIVE (female, siege commander)
- Xylos: CAPTURED (Ch 11)
- Dravos: ACTIVE (combat specialist)
- Two others: ACTIVE (not directly encountered in Book 1)

**Files Created**:
- `03-Reference/Demon-General-Status.md`

### 3. Aric's Experience Paradox (Chapters 4, 7)
**Issue**: Aric described as having "demon-fighting experience" but he's only ~40 and last war was 300 years ago
**Fix**: Changed references to emphasize Aric studied "ancient accounts" and "historical records"
- Chapter 4: "you have studied the ancient accounts more thoroughly than anyone living"
- Chapter 4: "According to the histories, demons feed on death..."
- Removed claims of personal demon-fighting experience

**Files Modified**:
- `Chapter 4 - Light in the Darkness.md` (lines 158-160, 186, 228)
- `Chapter 7 - The Grinder.md` (no direct Aric experience claims found)

### 4. Malakor Gender Inconsistency (Chapter 10)
**Issue**: Line 146 used "himself", but Malakor is consistently female throughout
**Fix**: Changed "General Malakor himself" to "General Malakor herself"

**Files Modified**:
- `Chapter 10 - The Long Retreat.md` (line 146)
