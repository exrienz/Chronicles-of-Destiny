## How to Use These Files

### When Writing Book 2

**Start Here:**
1. Read **Character-Status.md** - Know where every character is, their condition, relationships, and development
2. Read **World-Changes.md** - Understand how the world changed and what Book 2 inherits
3. Review **Book-Summary.md** - Refresh on plot events and progression

**Then:**
4. Create `Book-2-.../01-Planning/World-State-Book-2.md` based on this book's ending
5. Reference `00-Series/World-Building/Characters/` for detailed character profiles
6. Begin writing Book 2 with full continuity

### When Checking Continuity

Use **Character-Status.md** to verify:
- Is this character alive in Book 2?
- Where were they at end of Book 1?
- What was their emotional/physical state?
- What relationships had they formed?

Use **World-Changes.md** to verify:
- Does this kingdom still exist?
- Is this territory in Dome or outer lands?
- What factions are active?
- How has society changed?

## Important Notes

### Character Deaths (DO NOT RESURRECT CASUALLY)
- **Elfiria:** Died Chapter 14 from overusing divine power
- **Aric:** Died Chapter 8 fighting five Demon Generals
- **General Noxia:** Killed by Aric in Chapter 8

These deaths were significant story events. Resurrections would undermine their narrative weight.

### World Division
The world is now fundamentally divided:
- **Inner Lands:** Protected by Holy Dome (Aetheria, Eldoria, Sylvanaria, Drakoria)
- **Outer Lands:** Demon-controlled (Kharaz'Mar fallen, Luminara destroyed, Aridia conquered)

This is the new status quo for Book 2 and beyond.

### The Trinity
Kaelen, Lyra, and Drakon remain intact and are now legendary. This is a core relationship for the series.

### Power Progression
- **Kaelen:** Awakening Sword Saint abilities (NOT yet at Aric's level)
- **Holy Dome:** Active and protecting four kingdoms
- **Demon King:** Personally interested in Kaelen (corruption arc potential)
