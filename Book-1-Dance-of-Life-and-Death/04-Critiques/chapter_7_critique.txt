## 2. Narrative Critique

### Plot & Pacing
**Strengths:** The time jumps ("Six weeks later," "Eight weeks into campaign") effectively sell the "grind" of the war without bogged-down repetition. The introduction of specific tactical wins (supply lines) grounds the fantasy war in reality.
**Areas for Improvement:** The love triangle (Kaelen/Elfiria/Lyra) is now fully explicit. Lyra's reaction ("You look like a teenager") is a bit on-the-nose.
**Specific Suggestions:** Make Lyra's reaction more subtle. A lingering look, a forced smile, or just silence. Trust the reader to pick up on her feelings without her broadcasting them via "pain underneath" the dialogue.

### Character Development
**Strengths:** Kaelen's interaction with Aric ("I touched you") shows tangible growth in his skills.
**Areas for Improvement:** [FIXED] Drakon is becoming a caricature. His dialogue is almost entirely caps-lock declarations of joy/violence.
**Specific Suggestions:** Give Drakon a quiet moment. Maybe he mourns a fallen soldier in his own alien way, or shows intelligence in planning an ambush, not just executing it.
