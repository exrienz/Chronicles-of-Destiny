## 2. Narrative Critique

### Plot & Pacing
**Strengths:** The psychological toll of the siege ("Soldiers falling asleep at their posts," Line 68) is depicted well. The raid provides a necessary active beat in a passive siege story.
**Areas for Improvement:** [FIXED] Malakor's dialogue (Line 160) ("Delightfully audacious!", "Please try to entertain me!") is very villain-of-the-week. It lacks the gravitas of an ancient void entity.
**Specific Suggestions:** Make Malakor colder. Less "Game Show Host" and more "Bored God."
