CHAPTER 13 CRITIQUE

## 1. Fact-Checking & Continuity
*   **Evacuation Plausibility:** [FIXED] Evacuating 700 people (500 refugees + 200 fighters) through tunnels while under active assault by a Demon General and 300+ demons, with "Everyone who could be saved had been saved" (Line 220) implies a 100% survival rate for the civilians.
    *   *Critique:* This undermines the threat. If a Demon General breaches the walls, people should die. Panic, trampling, smoke inhalation, rear guard losses.
    *   *Suggestion:* Acknowledge losses. "We saved most. We didn't save all."

## 2. Narrative Critique

### Plot & Pacing
**Strengths:** The fall of Ironhold (Line 218) raises the stakes. It shows The Exiled aren't invincible and keeps them on the move, preventing the story from becoming a static tower defense.
**Areas for Improvement:** [FIXED] General Dravos (Line 126) enters the battle personally but fails to kill any main character or stop the evacuation.
**Specific Suggestions:** Have Dravos kill a named secondary character (maybe one of Grimm's lieutenants) to establish his threat level. Currently, he just talks and gets blocked.

### Character Development
**Strengths:** Kaelen's growth into a leader "not through official appointment but through demonstrated competence" (Line 240) is well-earned.
**Areas for Improvement:**
    1.  **Drakon:** "IS EXCELLENT BATTLE! ALSO PROBABLY WE DIE!" (Line 36).
    2.  **Elfiria:** [FIXED] Her sacrifice (Line 192) is dramatic, but she recovers enough to travel 5 miles immediately after (Line 224). The physical cost needs to be stickier.
**Specific Suggestions:**
    *   Have Elfiria be unconscious for days, or unable to walk at all, forcing them to build a litter.
