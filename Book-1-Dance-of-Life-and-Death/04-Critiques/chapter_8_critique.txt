### Character Development
**Strengths:** Kaelen stepping up to lead the council (Line 328) feels earned after Aric's mentorship.
**Areas for Improvement:**
    1.  **Drakon's Tone:** [FIXED] His dialogue during the massacre ("This is less excellent than previous battles!") is tonally jarring. It turns a horrific defeat into a joke.

**Specific Suggestions:**

    *   Have Kaelen sleep on the floor/chair in her tent as a guard, which achieves the same intimacy ("I won't leave you alone") without the impropriety.
