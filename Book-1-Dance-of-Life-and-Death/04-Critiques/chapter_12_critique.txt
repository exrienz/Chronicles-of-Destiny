## 2. Narrative Critique

### Plot & Pacing
**Strengths:** The shift from "Siege Defense" to "The Exiled" is a fantastic plot twist. It solves the stagnation of a long siege and gives the heroes agency again. The moral conflict (Safety vs. Compassion) drives the plot organically.
**Areas for Improvement:** [FIXED] "Zero Casualties" (Line 338). This phrase appears repeatedly (Ch 7, Ch 12).
**Specific Suggestions:** Stop letting them win perfectly. If they rescue 400 people, lose 5 fighters. War is messy. "Zero casualties" kills tension because the reader learns to expect perfect outcomes.

### Character Development
**Strengths:** King Aldric's private support (Line 122) adds nuance to what could have been a "Cowardly King" trope.
**Areas for Improvement:**
    1.  **Drakon:** "IS MOST GLORIOUS DECISION!" (Line 102). "IS SAME THING, DIFFERENT EMPHASIS!" (Line 106).
    2.  **Kaelen:** His "I'm coming with you" moment is expected but solid.
**Specific Suggestions:**
    *   Refine Drakon. He can be enthusiastic without shouting every line in caps. Use italics or exclamation marks sparingly.
