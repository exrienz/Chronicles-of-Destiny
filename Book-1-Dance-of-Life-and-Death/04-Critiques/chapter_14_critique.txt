CHAPTER 14 CRITIQUE

## 1. Fact-Checking & Continuity
*   **Magical Consistency:** [FIXED] Line 246 states Elfiria "unleashed the Holy Dome's full power" while in the canyon.
    *   *Critique:* In Chapter 11, the "Full Power" was the specific barrier around the kingdoms, activated at a specific temple. If she can just "unleash" it anywhere from the artifact, why is the barrier fixed?
    *   *Suggestion:* Change phrasing to "Unleashed the artifact's unchecked energy." Distinguish it from "The Dome" (which is the barrier).
*   **Zero Casualties:** Line 282. "Zero casualties."
    *   *Critique:* This is the third or fourth major battle with "Zero casualties." It makes the demons look incompetent. They are fighting 80 demons + 80 reinforcements = 160 demons vs 50 fighters. The demons should have killed at least *one* person.

## 2. Narrative Critique

### Plot & Pacing
**Strengths:** Elfiria's death is the emotional anchor of the book. It is handled well—not instant, allowing for good-byes, and focused on her legacy.
**Areas for Improvement:** The repetitive mission structure. Scout report -> "It's a trap" -> "We go anyway" -> "Zero casualties."
**Specific Suggestions:** Vary the mission. Maybe they get ambushed *en route*. Maybe they fail to save everyone. Maybe they have to retreat without the refugees.

### Character Development
**Strengths:** Drakon at the deathbed (Line 328) effectively drops the persona to show respect. "YOU ARE VERY BRAVE" works better here because it's sincere.
**Areas for Improvement:**
    1.  **Kaelen's Grief:** [FIXED] He recovers very quickly (Two weeks later, 'functioning'). While Lyra calls it out (Line 446), showing him failing during a mission due to grief would be more powerful than just "not sleeping."
**Specific Suggestions:**
    *   Have Kaelen hesitate in battle, almost getting hit, because he's distracted by grief.

### Prose & Style
