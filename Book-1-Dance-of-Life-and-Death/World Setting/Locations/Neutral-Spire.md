## Description

The Neutral Spire rises from the center of the Crossroads Plains "like an accusatory finger pointing at the sky." Built from white stone that predates current civilization, it is inscribed with wards so powerful that even the Demon King thinks twice about approaching.

Every kingdom has tried to claim the Spire at some point, failed spectacularly, and agreed to pretend it belongs to no one. This makes it the perfect neutral ground for diplomatic summits.

## Magical Properties

### Sentience
The Spire possesses rudimentary sentience and consciousness:
- **Detection:** Evaluates visitors for hostile intent
- **Response:** Acts "proportionally" to threats
- **Example:** Last attacker turned into decorative fountain (still functional)

### Neutral Magic
- **Type:** Neither void nor divine; pure neutral energy
- **Manifestation:** Central crystal in great hall
- **Effect:** Creates space where no faction has magical advantage

### Protective Wards
- **Power Level:** Strong enough to deter Demon King
- **Origin:** Unknown; pre-dates current civilizations
- **Function:** Prevents violence within Spire boundaries
