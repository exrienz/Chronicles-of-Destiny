## Description

Deephollow is an abandoned dwarven ruin that The Exiled transformed from rubble into something resembling hope made manifest. Located in mountainous terrain in the outer lands, it provides natural defenses and the advantages of dwarven underground engineering.

Over one year of occupation, The Exiled converted it from an empty ruin into a functioning resistance headquarters with manufacturing, agriculture, intelligence operations, and housing for fighters and refugees.

## Key Locations

### The Great Hall (Command Center)
- **Description:** Magnificent chamber carved from living stone
- **Original Purpose:** Dwarven ceremonial hall
- **Current Use:** War room and command center
- **Features:** Stone planning table, maps covering walls

### The Battlements
- **Description:** Stone walls with mountain views
- **Use:** Watch posts, morning contemplation
- **Notable:** Where Kaelen watches sunrise, plans operations

### Training Courtyard
- **Description:** Open area for combat drills
- **Capacity:** Accommodates hundreds of fighters
- **Schedule:** Morning drills daily

### Memorial Garden
- **Location:** Hill overlooking Deephollow
- **Description:** Ancient tree that survived demon corruption
- **Contents:** Elfiria's grave, flowers from every kingdom
- **Purpose:** Honor the fallen; Elfiria buried here per her request

### The Smithies (Lower Levels)
- **Operators:** Dwarven smiths
- **Products:** Void-resistant steel weapons
- **Process:** Tempering with holy water and crushed demon bone

### Underground Farms
- **Method:** Dwarven underground cultivation techniques
- **Magic:** Elfiria's residual purification magic
- **Challenge:** Finding uncorrupted crops in void-tainted lands
