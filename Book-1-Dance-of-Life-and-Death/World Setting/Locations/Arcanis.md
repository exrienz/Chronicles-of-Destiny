## Description

Arcanis is the gleaming capital of Eldoria, a massive city of white stone and ancient magic. Built in concentric circles with the Royal Palace at its heart, the city is protected by wards that have stood for five hundred years, inscribed into the very stone of its walls.

The city earned its title "City of Light" from the magical luminescence that emanates from its white stone at night, and from the presence of powerful mages who maintain its defenses.

## Architecture

### City Layout
- **Structure:** Concentric circles radiating from the Royal Palace
- **Walls:** Three stories of enchanted white stone
- **Gates:** Crowned with silver banner of Eldoria streaming in wind
- **Wards:** Protective inscriptions that have stood for 500 years

| District | Description |

## Key Locations

### Royal Palace
- Central position, highest point of the city
- Connected by floating bridges
- Seat of King Aldric
- War council chambers

### Gates of Arcanis
- Three stories of enchanted white stone
- Inscribed with protective wards (500 years old)
- Crowned with silver banner of Eldoria
- Become chokepoints during refugee crisis

### The Severed Cock
- Dive bar in outer slums
- Location for illicit dealings
- Where less reputable characters gather

### City Watch Headquarters
- Includes dungeon in basement
- Processing center for criminals

### Plaza of the First King
- Main public square
- Central fountain
- Gathering place for announcements

### Market District
- Chaotic, colorful merchant stalls
- Trade goods from across Eldoria

---

## Magical Defenses

### City Wards
- **Appearance:** Faint shimmer of protective magic
- **Function:** Burns demons attempting entry
- **Coverage:** Distinct layers - inner city has stronger protection
- **Origin:** Ancient magic inscribed into white stone
- **Duration:** Standing for 500 years

### Holy Dome (Chapter 11+)
- Divine barrier activated by Elfiria
- Protects Arcanis and four kingdoms
- Demons cannot penetrate
- Covers inner lands only

## Story Events

### Chapter 3: Chaos Descends
- Refugees flood the gates; processing overwhelmed
- Guards struggle with unprecedented volume of displaced people
- Every mage in sight collapses screaming when Demon King awakens
- Sky literally tears apart; void energy pours through
- City wards flare to life, holding back the darkness
- People outside the barrier transformed or erased

### Chapters 9-11: The Siege
- Demon army surrounds the city
- Daily assaults on the walls
- Void-Breaker supply raids to maintain resources
- Holy Dome activated from within the city

### Chapter 12: The Exiled Depart
- Kaelen, Lyra, Drakon, and others defy orders to leave
- The Exiled formally break from Alliance protection

---

## Status at End of Book 1

- **Condition:** Protected within Holy Dome
- **Population:** Increased (refugees from fallen kingdoms)
- **Military:** Rebuilding behind magical protection
- **Leadership:** King Aldric ruling; secretly funds The Exiled
- **Threat Level:** Safe but isolated; demons cannot breach Dome

## Atmosphere

### Before Demon King
- Political tension between kingdoms
- Rumors of war with Aridia
- Merchants and travelers passing through
- Normal city life with underlying unease

### After Demon King
- Under constant siege (Chapters 9-11)
- Refugees crowding streets
