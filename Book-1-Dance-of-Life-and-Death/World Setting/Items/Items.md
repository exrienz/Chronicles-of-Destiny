## LEGENDARY ARTIFACTS

### The Holy Dome
**Type:** Divine Artifact
**Origin:** Aetheria (celestial magic)
**Wielder:** Elfiria (High Priestess)

#### Description
A divine barrier artifact capable of protecting entire kingdoms from demonic incursion. When activated, it creates an impenetrable dome of celestial light that burns any demon attempting to cross.

#### Appearance
- **Active State:** Shimmering barrier of divine light
- **Coverage:** Four kingdoms (Aetheria, Eldoria, Sylvanaria, Drakoria)
- **Visual Effect:** Faint shimmer at boundaries; burns demons on contact

#### Properties
| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Protection** | Impenetrable to demons |
| **Coverage** | Four entire kingdoms |
| **Cost** | Drains wielder's life force |
| **Duration** | Permanent (as of Book 1 end) |

#### Cost to Wielder
- Every use burns portions of life force
- Connection to celestial realm weakens
- Pieces of soul consumed
- Elfiria died after 6 months of use (Chapter 14)

#### Story Events
- **Chapter 10:** Revealed as potential solution
- **Chapter 11:** Activated to protect inner kingdoms
- **Chapter 14:** Elfiria dies from overuse; Dome remains active

### Viewing Crystal
**Type:** Divine Scrying Device
**Location:** Elfiria's private chapel in Celestia

#### Description
A crystal device that allows observation of events across Mythosia through divine sight. Used by the High Priestess to monitor the world.

#### Properties
- Channels divine power for remote viewing
- Shows visions across entire continent
- Shattered when Demon King awakened (magical backlash)

#### Story Events
- **Chapter 3:** Elfiria uses it to witness Demon King's resurrection; shatters from void energy surge

## CHARACTER WEAPONS

### Kaelen's Twin Swords
**Type:** Standard Quality Dual Blades
**Wielder:** Kaelen (protagonist)

#### Description
A pair of twin swords won from a dead mercenary in Kharaz'Mar. Nothing special in themselves—good steel, decent balance, reliable. Their power comes from Kaelen's awakening Sword Saint abilities.

#### Acquisition
Won from mercenary who died taking contract against dragon cult

#### Properties
| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Quality** | Good steel, decent balance |
| **Style** | Dual-wielding matched pair |
| **Special** | Silver energy manifests when Echo State active |
| **Maintenance** | Always within reach; sheaths whisper when drawn |

#### Awakened State (Echo State)
When Kaelen channels his awakening power:
- Silver energy coats the blades
- Enhanced cutting power
- Supernatural speed
