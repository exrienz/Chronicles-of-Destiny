## General Dravos - Herald of War

**Status:** ALIVE & ACTIVE (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of War
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 8

### Physical Appearance
- Giant demon, armor fused to his skin
- Wields tombstone-sized claymore
- Radiates war energy

### Abilities
- **War Aura:** Increases lethality of all combat nearby
- **Overwhelming Strength:** Caught Aric's "Final Horizon" strike barehanded
- **Combat Mastery:** Centuries of warfare experience
- **Soul Blade:** Weapon leaves screaming soul afterimages
- **Respects Strength:** Acknowledges worthy opponents

### Major Events (Book 1)
- **Chapter 8:** Caught Aric's final attack; delivered killing blow through Aric's chest with claymore
- **Chapter 13:** Led assault on Ironhold fortress; killed Thane Borin; dueled Kaelen (both wounded)
- **Chapter 15:** Part of Malakor's ambush on Kaelen's strike team

### Status at End of Book 1
- **Condition:** Healthy and active
- **Location:** Outer lands
- **Threat Level:** HIGH - extremely dangerous in direct combat

## General Xylos - Herald of Pain/Suffering

**Status:** CAPTURED (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of Pain (also called Herald of Suffering)
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 8

### Abilities
- **Psychic Scream:** Killed 50 soldiers with single mental attack
- **Pain Amplification:** Supernatural agony inducement
- **Regeneration:** Survived being cut in half by Aric
- **Void Magic:** Shadow/void abilities

### Major Events (Book 1)
- **Chapter 8:** Unleashed psychic attack at Valley of Kings; cut in half by Aric's blade (regenerated)
- **Chapter 11:** CAPTURED during Void-Breaker supply raid at Temple of Stars

### Status at End of Book 1
- **Condition:** CAPTURED - held by Alliance within Holy Dome
- **Location:** Alliance custody (under interrogation)
- **Threat Level:** NEUTRALIZED while contained
- **Note:** First Demon General captured alive; significant intelligence asset

## General Vex - Herald of Plague

**Status:** CRITICALLY WOUNDED (as of Book 1 ending)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of Plague
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 8

### Abilities
- **Plague Breath:** Miasma clouds that melt flesh
- **Disease Manipulation:** Creates supernatural plagues
- **Slow Regeneration:** Can recover from extreme damage

### Status at End of Book 1
- **Condition:** Disintegrated but slowly regenerating from ash
- **Location:** Unknown (demon territory)
- **Threat Level:** REDUCED - may take considerable time to fully recover

## General Noxia - Herald of Shadows

**Status:** DECEASED (killed Chapter 8)

### Basic Information
- **Title:** Herald of Shadows
- **Race:** Demon
- **Rank:** One of Seven Demon Generals
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 8
- **Death:** Book 1, Chapter 8

### Abilities
- **Shadow Travel:** Could emerge from any shadow, including target's own shadow
- **Assassination:** Specialized in surprise attacks
- **Shadow Magic:** Master of darkness manipulation

### Death
- **How:** Stabbed Aric in the kidney from his own shadow; vaporized by Aric's "Final Horizon" technique
- **Killed By:** Aric (Sword Saint)
- **Location:** Valley of Kings battlefield

### Legacy
- First Demon General confirmed killed
- Death proved Generals CAN be permanently destroyed
- Aric traded his life to destroy her
