# King Aldric of Eldoria

**Category:** Supporting Character - Political Leader
**Status:** Alive (as of Book 1 ending)

## Basic Information

- **Full Name:** King Aldric
- **Race:** Human
- **Age:** 50s
- **Kingdom:** Eldoria (King/Ruler)
- **Affiliation:** Alliance of Light (Co-leader)
- **First Appearance:** Book 1, Chapter 3

## Physical Appearance

- **Beard:** Gray
- **Eyes:** Sharp, intelligent
- **Physical Changes:** Visibly ages throughout the war from stress
- **Bearing:** Royal but weary from burden of leadership

## Personality

- **Pragmatic:** Makes decisions based on practical reality
- **Burdened:** Carries weight of crown and people's survival
- **Decisive:** Can make hard choices when necessary
- **Survival-Focused:** Prioritizes kingdom's survival over idealism
- **Conflicted:** Struggles between duty and morality

## Leadership Role

- **King of Eldoria:** Rules one of five Alliance kingdoms
- **Alliance Co-Leader:** Shares leadership with other monarchs
- **Military Oversight:** Works with Aric on military strategy
- **Political Navigation:** Manages complex Alliance politics

## Major Events (Book 1)

- **Chapter 3:** Responds to emergency summit; helps form Alliance of Light
- **Chapter 4:** Attends Neutral Spire; agrees to unified command
- **Chapters 5-10:** Leads Eldoria's contribution to Alliance war effort
- **Chapter 12:** Approves Holy Dome activation despite leaving Kharaz'Mar outside
- **Chapter 14:** Allows The Exiled to leave; secretly provides funding
- **Chapter 15:** Rules protected Eldoria within Dome; quietly supports Exiled

## Relationship with Kaelen

- **Respectful:** Recognizes Kaelen's growing importance
- **Conflicted:** Understands Kaelen's moral stance but chooses pragmatism
- **Secret Support:** Provides funds to The Exiled despite official neutrality

## Key Decisions

1. Joined Alliance of Light against demons
2. Approved Holy Dome activation (sacrificing outer kingdoms)
3. Allowed Exiled to leave rather than imprison them
4. Secretly funds The Exiled resistance

## Status at End of Book 1

- **Living Status:** Alive
- **Position:** King of Eldoria within Holy Dome
- **Responsibility:** Protecting and rebuilding Eldoria
- **Secret Action:** Quietly supporting The Exiled with resources
