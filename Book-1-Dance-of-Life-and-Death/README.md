# Book 1: The Dance of Life and Death

**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE (15 chapters)

## Overview

The first book of Chronicles of Destiny. Follows Kaelen from cynical mercenary to leader of The Exiled resistance.

### Key Themes
- Sacrifice and choice
- Love in war
- Found family (The Trinity)
- Heroism reborn
- Moral complexity of the Holy Dome decision
